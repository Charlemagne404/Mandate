# Remaining development

The alpha now integrates desktop launch, world-scale synthetic content, constrained providers/roles, multi-government planning, private diplomacy, autonomous activation, ongoing projects, coarse strategic conflict/economy/domestic mechanics, factual retrieval, snapshots/branching/rollback and causal inspection.

Next milestone: evaluate real local models on the integrated 242-polity game. Measure arbitrary-action interpretation, independent recipient behavior, semantic resolution quality, compound secrecy, long-memory continuity and latency. Tune role prompts/models/context only against those recorded results; add adversarial regressions for actual failures. Fake-provider success cannot certify model quality.

Then deepen gameplay against explicit deterministic rules: multilateral settlement/support, theater/projection logistics, sanctions/trade mechanisms, cause-driven political transitions, structured goal progress/evidence, intelligence discovery and persistence of long-term summaries. Expand scenario authoring and improve long-history SQL/retrieval/snapshot costs with measurements.

Native release work remains signed/notarized macOS distribution, Windows/Linux runtime/packaging tests, dependency notices review for distribution, and the owner's selection of a project license. Province-level historical borders, tactical units, multiplayer, accounts and cloud services are outside this alpha's priority.
