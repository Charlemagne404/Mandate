# Deterministic alpha mechanics

World schema and save/scenario format version 2 add initiatives, negotiations and international organizations. Version 1 files migrate through the import boundary, with neutral defaults for added dimensions and empty added collections. Validation and invariants still run after migration. SQLite migration `002_alpha.sql` adds metadata columns, canonical entity tables, organization membership rows, event effects and supplemental turn audits. Migration `003_settlements.sql` adds foreign-key conflict links on treaties and negotiations without changing applied migration checksums. Newly authored alpha schema 2 includes defaulted conflict settlement state and nullable settlement links. The existing ledger and political rows survive a v1 database upgrade.

Nation indices are dimensionless 0–100 values: economy, military, stability, legitimacy, industrial, fiscal, readiness, manpower, technology, influence, energy exposure, trade dependence and unrest. Treasury remains an abstract balance capped at one billion. These are scenario abstractions, not estimates of actual GDP, soldiers or casualties. Bilateral records retain score and add trust, tension, trade dependence, military alignment and grievances. Conflicts add exhaustion, logistics and qualitative war goals. Strategic goals add kind, visibility, deadline, blockers and evidence; their creation command and later updates provide provenance.

## Initiatives and passage of time

`START_INITIATIVE` creates an active project with an owner, type, duration, effort, optional target, visibility and dependencies. Industry, energy, rearmament, reform, diplomacy and aid have distinct completion effects. Projects start at the current simulation date with no progress or investment. Aid requires another nation. Dependencies must already exist and share the owner. Equivalent active projects are rejected. `CANCEL_INITIATIVE` preserves invested cost and historical state and prevents completion benefits.

`ADVANCE_DATE` drives fixed 30-day accounting ticks anchored to the scenario start. Splitting an advance into smaller commands does not create extra economic ticks. A single advance is bounded to 1,200 accounting ticks. Treasury receives abstract economic/fiscal income; active war creates military burden, economic disruption, readiness decay, unrest and conflict exhaustion. Peaceful, sufficiently stable industrial nations converge gradually toward industrial/fiscal economic potential, capped at 95 rather than growing indefinitely. High unrest erodes stability and legitimacy; legitimate peaceful governments gradually reduce unrest.

On eligible accounting ticks, an initiative purchases one installment using treasury. At least 30 simulation days must elapse before its first installment. An unfunded or dependency-blocked project stalls. Progress reflects actual investment, not elapsed time alone. Completion applies bounded effects once. Requested durations round up to 30-day installments, and completion waits for the next accounting tick; there can be up to 29 additional days of scheduling delay. Aid transfers invested resources to its target on completion.

Negotiations expire on the requested advance date even between accounting ticks. Active goals with elapsed deadlines become abandoned and retain an explicit deadline blocker. The engine does not invent coups or elections when time advances; domestic pressure currently affects indices and can motivate subsequent government decisions.

## Negotiations

`OPEN_NEGOTIATION` creates an offer between two known, distinct governments with terms, a topic, visibility and expiry. Supported kinds are consultation, defense, trade, nonaggression, ceasefire and peace. Equivalent open offers between the same parties are rejected. The same negotiation object retains all responses.

`RESPOND_NEGOTIATION` permits the current recipient to accept, reject, counter or delay; the current proposer can withdraw. A counter must provide new terms and swaps proposer/recipient. A delay preserves the original expiry. Rejection, withdrawal and expiration close the conversation. No closed conversation can be accepted later. Conflict settlement offers require a known active conflict with exactly one government on each opposing side; those governments must be the offer parties. Other offer kinds cannot link a conflict.

Accepting consultation records agreement to consultation without creating a treaty. Binding acceptance requires a fresh TreatyId and creates the canonical treaty inside the same command and atomic turn. Treaty kind/parties derive from the accepted offer. An equivalent active treaty rejects the entire turn. Prose alone creates no commitment. Private offers produce private response events and private treaties; both negotiation participants can retrieve the conversation. Private initiatives and strategic goals disclose their events only to the owner, even when another nation is their target.

## Organizations and strategic conflict

`CREATE_ORGANIZATION` creates an alliance, economic organization, institution or regional forum with a charter and unique known members. `SET_ORGANIZATION_MEMBERSHIP` explicitly joins or leaves; an organization must retain at least one member. Membership currently describes a geopolitical relationship and does not automatically invoke war obligations.

`CONFLICT_ACTION` requires an active-conflict participant. Mobilization and reinforcement cost treasury and improve readiness; reinforcement and defense improve the conflict's abstract logistics. De-escalation reduces escalation. Offensives require readiness, treasury and an enemy-controlled region. A deterministic capacity/readiness/logistics comparison decides whether military control changes; both successful and repelled attacks consume resources and increase exhaustion/escalation. Ownership and permanent geometry never change through combat. There are no tactical units, casualties, adjacency routes, theater logistics, external military aid or automatic peace settlements yet. Treat this as a coarse strategic alpha mechanic, not a complete warfare simulation.

Accepted ceasefire offers create linked treaties and change the active conflict to `settlementState: ceasefire`. Offensive actions are legally blocked. Accounting charges reduced military maintenance, avoids combat-driven readiness decay and unrest, and gradually reduces exhaustion. Defense/mobilization remain available. Explicitly ending the ceasefire treaty restores fighting; it does not silently transfer territory. Direct settlement creation through `CREATE_TREATY` and arbitrary settlement-term edits are rejected.

Accepted peace offers create linked peace treaties, end the conflict, end its active ceasefire agreement and close any outstanding settlement offers through recorded withdrawal responses. The treaty's canonical mechanical terms explicitly retain current military control and unchanged legal ownership. No prose term can adjudicate annexation or ownership transfer. Peace can follow a ceasefire or be agreed directly. A subsequent explicit `END_CONFLICT` also closes obsolete settlement offers and active ceasefire treaties. These transitions, references and agreement/conflict correspondence are validated on imports. Multilateral settlement and territorial bargaining remain a later mechanic.

`APPLY_DOMESTIC_PRESSURE` increases unrest and reduces stability with an explicit cause. Government and leader changes remain existing narrow commands. No generic entity patch command exists.

## Provenance, persistence and boundaries

Every accepted command produces its factual event and ledger entry. Event effects record actual before/after nation indices computed from deterministic application; models cannot submit this field through `CREATE_EVENT`. Time-derived consequences point to the `ADVANCE_DATE` command. Supplemental model traces commit in `turn_audits` inside the same SQLite transaction; a trace-write failure rolls back all state, time, actions and facts. Trace JSON is bounded and has no mechanical authority.

Named timeline snapshots can retain traces alongside canonical saves and restore them with validated, known turn references in the same import transaction. Plain JSON save export contains canonical world/history, not supplemental model traces. Foreign references, duplicate entities, dependency cycles, invalid progress/investment, chronology errors and mismatched accepted treaties fail import validation. Provider, Node, SQL and Electron imports remain absent from the deterministic core.

Tests cover v1 database migration, v2 save round trips, funding/dependencies/cancellation/completion, actor-authorized diplomatic transitions, consultation versus binding acceptance, secrecy, expiration, organizations, strategic combat outcomes, economic/domestic/war passage of time, split-time equivalence, rollback and trace restoration. These mechanics use bounded deterministic arithmetic; no randomness or model result silently decides a canonical transition.
