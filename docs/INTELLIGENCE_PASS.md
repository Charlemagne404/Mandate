This is the previous gameplay-depth cycle. Current implementation, verification and remaining limitations are in [WORLD_CONTINUITY_PASS.md](WORLD_CONTINUITY_PASS.md).

### Observed runs and fixes

The 25-turn Nordic run had 27 completed projects, no completed goals yet, no rapid cooldown violations and no invariant failures. The 50-turn run completed four goals and 55 projects; about 13.7% of meaningful events involved Sweden; all 13 actors planned with maximum gap seven. Averages were roughly 39 ms/turn at 25 turns and 46 ms/turn at 50 in the demo. Repeated programs remain possible after cooldown when target signals still require investment.

The first 100-turn global attempt aborted safely at turn 17 when crisis activation duplicated a rotating background actor. A regression now checks uniqueness across thirty scheduler revisions. Subsequent runs exposed exhausted countries endlessly countering peace because an earlier hostile plan overrode settlement economics. The demo decision priority was corrected; a later 100-turn run ended the existing conflict with an independently accepted peace treaty. It completed 200 projects, had no territorial changes or invariant failures, activated 202 of 242 actors, and averaged about 403 ms/turn including in-memory SQLite commits, hashing, traces and metrics. Maximum context was about 31k characters. These are demo timings, not real inference latency. The full global rotation requires more than 100 turns at two background slots.

The final global run recorded 550 model calls with zero generation failures or repairs, 511 meaningful events, zero player involvement and no rapid project cooldown violations. Repetition vetoes reduced repeated global event signatures from 265 in an earlier run to 45; residual repeated negotiations and routine history remain visible.

Two important warnings remain: global generic legacy goals without progress signals never complete, and autonomous consultation offers were universally accepted in that run. Those observations are retained in reports; passing contract tests does not erase them. The 50-turn Nordic run is richer than the generic global baseline.

The relevant 250-turn deterministic fixture and short global integrity soaks are included in the unit suite. The previous alpha's 1,000-turn result is historical evidence; it was not repeated as the primary objective of this cycle. No real-model 5/10/25/50-turn simulation ran.

## Implemented but shallow

- Country personality is mostly structured priorities/constraints plus bounded demo judgment. Real-model national-interest quality, nuanced concessions and context usage remain untested. Government/leader change does not yet revise policy orientation.
- Goals with explicit stat signals and children have meaningful progress; legacy goals without signals remain coarse. Lifecycle labels do not constitute a complete proposal/adoption/supersession workflow.
- Commitments mechanically cover funded aid, domestic project delivery and restraint. Treaty assent alone does not create every possible obligation. The advisor cannot yet comprehensively answer commitment/fiscal questions.
- Relations store recent causes (bounded forty factors), not a permanent full decomposition of the entire score. Dependencies are symmetric general trade dependence and national energy exposure, not a commodity network.
- Economy has funding, capacity, war expense and trade opportunity costs; no debt, sanctions network, detailed fiscal accounts or country-specific energy suppliers.
- Campaigns are strategic progress against permanent country-sized regions, with deterministic strength resolution; no stochastic combat, per-side exhaustion, theater logistics network, blockade or external aid combat modifier.
- Peace supports status quo, withdrawal and transfer. No richer multilateral bargain or representation of reparations, demilitarization or humanitarian provisions.
- Repetition detection uses typed action identity, cooldown and normalized terms; it does not detect all semantic paraphrases or measure incentive changes. Demo adaptation recognizes prior refusal but does not implement a sophisticated alternative-partner search.
- Retrieval retains important older events and active structured obligations. Event selection has a character budget, but all canonical collections are not yet dynamically compacted; over-budget calls still fail safely. No embeddings introduced.
- Importance is a lexical/active-conflict classifier, not a learned judge. One model remains valid. Model health enumerates names but not server versions/digests.
- Turn summaries and major ledger entries expose meaningful transitions; a dedicated dismissible priority-notification inbox and rich result hierarchy remain incomplete.
- The existing postcommit narrator fallback/cancellation and source Electron smoke remain reliable; comprehensive process-kill injection through each generation phase was not added.

## Designed but not implemented

Structured schema vocabulary reserves guarantee, basing and peace-term commitment types, but domain validation deliberately rejects those unsupported obligation conditions. They need enforceable triggers and negotiated fulfillment mechanics before use. The documented playtest includes future multilateral conference expectations but explicitly uses bilateral mechanics today. No claim of those features working is made.

## Not attempted

Multilateral negotiating procedure; semantic branch comparison; a new canonical advisor; persistent crisis-chain entities; government change/election dynamics; structured DEEP government deliberation; automatic context caching; safe parallel diplomacy calls; advanced trade/sanctions/resources; richer war/peace terms; embeddings; tactical breadth; real-model soaks without an endpoint. No source or alpha guarantees were replaced for these deferrals.
